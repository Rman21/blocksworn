# Privacy Policy — Blocksworn

**Last updated:** 2026-05-02
**Effective date:** [SOFT_LAUNCH_DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how Blocksworn ("we", "us", "the Game") collects, uses, and shares information when you play the game on iOS, Android, or web.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide directly
- **None.** Blocksworn does not require account creation, email, or phone number to play.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Anonymous user ID** — generated by Firebase Anonymous Auth, used to sync your save across sessions on the same device.
- **Game progression** — saved locally to your device via `localStorage` and synced to Firebase Firestore so progress survives reinstalls (when the same anonymous ID resolves).
- **Purchase history** — when you make an in-app purchase, we record the SKU, transaction ID, and amount via RevenueCat (our payment processor) and Apple/Google's payment systems.
- **Crash reports** — when the app crashes, we send the error message + sanitized stack trace to Sentry. No personal data is included.
- **Analytics events** — game events (boss defeats, shop opens, purchase intents, retention markers) are sent to Firebase Analytics in aggregate cohorts. Examples: `boss_defeated`, `purchase_started`, `d7_return`. No personal data is included.
- **Device information** — operating system, browser type, screen resolution (for adaptive UI). Not tied to identity.

### 1.3 Information we DO NOT collect
- Real name, email, or phone number
- Location data (beyond your device's timezone, used only for daily resets)
- Contact lists, calendar, photos, or camera
- Browsing history outside the game

---

## 2. How We Use Information

- **Provide the service:** sync your save, deliver purchased content, render leaderboards.
- **Improve the game:** analyze aggregate retention, identify crashes, balance economy via cohort funnels.
- **Process purchases:** validate receipts via RevenueCat + Apple/Google; handle refunds.
- **Customer support:** if you email support@blocksworm.com, we respond using whatever information you provide in the email.

We do **not** use your data for advertising profiling, sell to third parties, or share with data brokers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase (Google) | Anonymous auth, save sync, analytics, crashes | UID, save data, event names |
| RevenueCat | Purchase processing | Transaction ID, SKU, price |
| Apple App Store | iOS payment processing | Apple ID (handled by Apple, not us) |
| Google Play Store | Android payment processing | Google account (handled by Google, not us) |
| Sentry | Crash + performance monitoring | Error messages, sanitized stack traces |
| Stripe | Web payment processing (desktop browser) | Card details (handled by Stripe, not us) |

Each third party has their own privacy policy. We minimize what we send to each.

---

## 4. Data Retention

- **Save data:** retained as long as your anonymous Auth account is active (on your device). If your device is wiped or you reinstall after Apple/Google clear the anonymous ID, the save becomes inaccessible.
- **Purchase records:** retained for 7 years (US tax compliance) or as required by Apple/Google.
- **Crash reports:** auto-deleted by Sentry after 90 days (free tier).
- **Analytics data:** auto-deleted by Firebase Analytics after 14 months.

You can request deletion at any time (see §6).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Blocksworn is rated **12+** (Apple) / **Teen** (Google). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (US) or under 16 (EU). If you believe a child has provided data, contact privacy@blocksworm.com — we will delete it immediately.

---

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 GDPR (EU residents)
- **Access:** request a copy of your data (email privacy@blocksworm.com).
- **Deletion:** tap "Delete Account" in Profile settings — this triggers a Cloud Function that removes your Firestore data + Auth account. Local save also wiped. Irreversible.
- **Portability:** request your data in JSON format.
- **Objection:** opt out of analytics in Profile settings (sets local flag preventing event dispatch).

### 6.2 CCPA (California residents)
- **Right to know:** what we collect (see §1).
- **Right to delete:** see §6.1 deletion process.
- **Right to opt out of sale:** we do not sell data. Always opted out by default.
- **Right to non-discrimination:** opting out does not change game functionality (paid features still work).

### 6.3 Other jurisdictions
Where local law grants you privacy rights not listed above, we honor them. Email privacy@blocksworm.com.

---

## 7. Security

- All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS.
- Firestore data at rest is encrypted by Google.
- Payment card details never touch our servers (handled by Apple/Google/Stripe).
- We do not store any credentials.

No system is perfectly secure. If you suspect a breach affecting your account, email security@blocksworm.com.
