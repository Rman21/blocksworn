# Sprint 6 — Apple / Google Relationship Building

**Source:** SPRINT_6_FULL_LAUNCH_PREP.md §7
**Use:** Long-game playbook. Featuring is a months-long courtship, not a launch-day request.

---

## Why platform relationships matter

```
Apple "Game of the Day" featuring:    50K-500K downloads in 1-3 days
Google Play "Editor's Choice":        30K-300K downloads in 1-3 days

Combined value: $200K-1.5M in install equivalent (vs paid CAC)
Featured games show ~25% higher long-term retention (trust signal)
```

---

## What platforms look for

### Apple
- **Polish ranks above content in Apple's eyes**
- Native iOS feel (Capacitor wrap is fine, but UI must feel iOS)
- Privacy nutrition label honesty
- In-app purchase quality (no manipulative dark patterns)
- Localization (more languages = more featuring slots)
- Apple-specific features (iCloud sync, Game Center, ARKit, etc. — bonus)

### Google
- Performance metrics (crash rate, ANR rate)
- Engaging gameplay
- Inclusive design
- Material Design alignment (less strict than Apple)
- Google-specific features (Play Games Services, etc. — bonus)
- Tablet optimization

## Timeline

### Pre-launch (Month 0)

```
□ Apply for Apple Developer Program ($99/year)
□ Apply for Google Play Developer ($25 one-time)
□ Submit product to App Review (~24-48h review)
```

### Soft launch (Sprint 5)

```
□ Engage Apple/Google email channels for support questions only
□ Track crash rates, ANR rates, performance metrics
□ Build clean review history with testers (no negative manipulation)
```

### Public launch (Sprint 6 Day 0)

```
□ Submit app to platform team for "preferred game" consideration via:
  - Apple: App Store Connect → "Promote your app" form
  - Google: Play Console → standard submission process
□ Don't expect immediate response (3-6 month process)
```

### Days 30-90

```
□ Monitor for featuring opportunities
□ If featured: be ready for traffic surge (server scaling)
□ Respond promptly to platform team requests
□ Quality over quantity in updates (don't spam updates)
```

### Long-term

```
□ Cultivate relationship with platform team via official channels
□ Submit to "preferred game" pitches at WWDC, Google I/O, etc.
□ Track what platform features become available; integrate when valuable
```

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Email Apple/Google daily asking for featuring (annoys them)
- ❌ Submit excessive update versions (signal of instability)
- ❌ Engage in review manipulation (banned from the platform)
- ❌ Bypass platform IAP (banned)
- ❌ Post fake reviews (banned)
- ❌ Send direct messages on Discord/social to platform team (unprofessional)

---

## Featuring application timing

Don't apply on Day 0. Wait until **Day 30+** when stability is proven.

If denied, re-apply after 90+ days with measurable improvements:
- Higher rating
- Lower crash rate
- New content (e.g., next chapter)
- Localization additions
