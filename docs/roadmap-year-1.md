## How we work

- **Battle Pass:** new season every 60 days. No skipping.
- **Polish patches:** monthly, bug fixes + quality of life
- **Major content:** every ~90 days (chapters, race expansions, Tower variants)
- **Weekly events:** Wednesday Chest tiers, weekend tournaments, friend referral pulses
- **Communication:** changelog goes to Discord first, then in-app banner for major drops

---

## How to influence the roadmap

- Discord `#feedback` channel — read daily
- App Store reviews — read weekly
- Friend system messages — when relevant
- Direct: `support@blocksworm.com`

We don't promise we'll act on every request, but we read all of them and the roadmap shifts based on what the community is hungry for.
