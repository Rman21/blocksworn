## Filling-in tips

- **Don't sandbag the variance column** — tell the truth
- **"What didn't" is the most valuable section** — sunk-cost denial is the enemy
- Variance > ±20% requires a written explanation, not a blank
- Path commitment in "Decisions" is binding for the next 90 days; don't waffle
- Year 2 prelim view is high-level only (detailed Year 2 calendar locks at Month 12)
