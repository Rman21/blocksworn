## Path 1 — Organic ($0-5K)

**Channels:**

1. **App Store Optimization (ASO)** — primary lever
   - Title + subtitle keywords aligned to "puzzle RPG"
   - Screenshot quality + ordering
   - Long description hooks
   - Keyword research: appfigures or Sensor Tower (free tier)
   - Iterate every 14 days based on search ranking

2. **Reddit** — community-driven discovery
   - r/MobileGaming AMAs (1-2 per quarter)
   - r/AndroidGaming / r/iOSGaming announcement posts
   - Genuine engagement (NO promotional spam)
   - Cross-post from Discord

3. **Discord community growth**
   - Soft-launch Discord = base
   - Add-friend bonuses encourage referrals
   - Weekly events (Tower leaderboards, fan art)
   - Roman participates daily for first 30 days

4. **TikTok organic** (high-effort, high-leverage)
   - 3-5 short videos per week
   - Topics: hero reveals, boss fights, Tower runs, behind-the-scenes
   - Goal: viral moment (low probability, high reward)

**Budget:** $0-5K total
- Domain renewal: ~$50
- ASO tool subscription: ~$30/mo
- Misc graphics: $200-1K

**Expected:**
- Year 1 installs: 30K-80K organic
- Year 1 revenue: $30K-80K
- Year 1: break-even or small loss

## Cross-cutting principles

### DO
- ✅ Track ROAS by channel daily, kill underperformers fast
- ✅ Always give Apple/Google their honest cut — no try at workarounds
- ✅ Test creative variants weekly (pause winning ones BEFORE fatigue)
- ✅ Use UTM parameters or Adjust/AppsFlyer for attribution
- ✅ Save 20-30% of budget as reserve for opportunity / crisis
- ✅ Brand creative (consistent visual identity across channels)

### DON'T
- ❌ Spend equally across channels at launch (some won't work — concentrate)
- ❌ Run ads claiming features that don't exist
- ❌ Promise launch dates publicly that you might miss
- ❌ Spend all budget month 1 (12-month commitment minimum)
- ❌ Ignore CAC vs LTV math (CAC must be < LTV)

## Apple Search Ads quick start (Path 2/3)

```
1. Start with branded keywords: "blocksworn"
2. Add competitor keywords: "royal match", "afk arena", "marvel snap"
3. Bid carefully — start low, raise based on conversion
4. Use Search Match (Apple's auto-keyword) to discover opportunities
5. A/B test custom product pages (different screenshots per keyword set)
6. Target CPI: $2-4 for casual+core hybrid genre
```
