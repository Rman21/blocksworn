## Day 90 trajectory check

By Day 90, the game's trajectory should be classifiable. Pick the closest landing zone, then act:

| Trajectory | D30 retention | ARPDAU | Year 1 estimate | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 7-10% | $0.05-0.07 | $50-100K | Continue Path 1, Year 2 plan = "indie sustainable" |
| **Base** | **10-13%** | **$0.07-0.10** | **$300-500K** | **Continue Path 2, Year 2 plan = "scale + team"** |
| Optimistic | 13%+ | $0.10+ | $1M+ | Continue Path 3, Year 2 plan = "studio scale" |
| Below conservative | <7% | <$0.05 | <$50K | **Major decision — sustain or sunset** |

---

## Below-trajectory options (if D30 retention < 7%)

```
1. SUSTAIN
   - Reduce burn to match revenue
   - Continue minimal polish + monthly content
   - Year 1 budget: minimal team, lean operation

2. PIVOT
   - Major game design changes
   - Re-soft-launch with adjustments
   - Risk: 6-month delay before public re-launch

3. SUNSET
   - Honest assessment: not enough traction
   - Continue server costs at break-even
   - Use as portfolio piece, focus on next game
```

**Decision criteria:**
- Roman's emotional investment (sunk cost recognition)
- Capital available for sustained development
- Trajectory vs alternatives (other games to make)

---

## Above-trajectory opportunities (if D30 retention 12%+)

```
□ Promote Path → Path 2 → Path 3 if capital available
□ Hire team (1-3 contractors → full-time)
□ Accelerate content cadence (next chapter sooner)
□ Localization expansion
□ Esports / tournament features
□ Possible publisher conversation
□ Apple/Google featuring follow-up
□ PR campaign for Year 1 success
```

**Warning:**
- Don't over-extend on success
- Maintain reserves for inevitable downswings
- Year 2 plan should be ambitious but realistic
- Avoid "raise too fast, expand too fast" mistake
